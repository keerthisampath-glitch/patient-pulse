        About PatientPulse AI

        Enterprise Multimodal Clinical Decision Support & Zero-Jargon Patient Intelligence Platform

        ● Platform v1.0.0 • Production Ready

        Our Clinical Mission

        Diagnostic medical data is notoriously complex, highly fragmented, and laden with clinical jargon. A patient receiving a radiograph report reading "Cardiomegaly with bilateral blunting of costophrenic angles", a scribbled prescription slip for "Amoxicillin 500mg TDS", or a lab blood panel with flagged AST/ALT transaminases is often left confused, anxious, and at risk of non-compliance.

        PatientPulse AI bridges this critical communication divide. By unifying PyTorch deep learning vision, transformer-based handwriting OCR, biomedical language processing (BioBERT), and grounded government medical retrieval (NIH MedlinePlus & openFDA), the platform translates intimidating clinical artifacts into clear, transparent, and actionable guidance for patients while providing attending physicians with rapid, high-precision diagnostic triage.

        Core AI Models & Neural Architecture

        Five specialized, production-grade intelligence engines powering PatientPulse AI:

        🩻

        Universal Radiograph Vision

        TorchXRayVision
        Primary Model: DenseNet-121 pre-trained on NIH ChestX-ray14 + Gemini Multimodal Vision Fallback
        • Screens 18 pulmonary pathologies (Cardiomegaly, Pneumonia, Atelectasis, Effusion, Infiltration, etc.).
        • Evaluates skeletal & joint trauma (fractures, dislocations, osteo-degenerative changes).
        • Calculates normalized bounding-box coordinates over detected anatomical abnormalities.
        • Generates transparent RGBA Grad-CAM activation heatmaps for clinical interpretability.
        📝

        Prescription Handwriting OCR

        Microsoft TrOCR
        Primary Model: `microsoft/trocr-base-handwritten` + PyMuPDF Layout Parser
        • Deciphers cursive and notoriously illegible doctor prescription handwriting slips and PDFs.
        • Constructs an intuitive 4-slot daily timetable (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Bedtime).
        • Issues prominent clinical alerts whenever character confidence falls below 80%.
        • Cross-references medication names against openFDA drug databases for food-drug interaction warnings.
        🧪

        Biomarker NLP & Lab Explainer

        Bio_ClinicalBERT
        Primary Model: `emilyalsentzer/Bio_ClinicalBERT` + Clinical Biomarker Graph
        • Parses 30+ blood metrics (CBC, Lipid Panel, Liver Function, Renal Function, Metabolic Panel).
        • Automated triage: classifies every value into NORMAL, HIGH, LOW, or CRITICAL.
        • Generates dual-level explanations: "What It Does" and "What Your Result Means" in simple English.
        • Formulates tailored Questions for Your Doctor to empower informed patient-physician dialogue.
        📚

        Grounded Medical RAG

        NIH & openFDA
        Primary Sources: Live NIH MedlinePlus REST + openFDA Labeling REST + ChromaDB
        • Zero Hallucination Policy: Queries live official databases for every clinical claim.
        • Fetches real-time FDA Boxed Warnings, contraindications, and active drug ingredients.
        • Retrieves authoritative NIH MedlinePlus medical encyclopedic definitions.
        • Embeds verified government links (`nlm.nih.gov` & `fda.gov`) directly into every response.
        🤖

        24/7 AI Health Companion Agent

        Groq Llama 3.1 & Gemini
        Primary Architecture: Groq Llama 3.1 8B Instant (sub-second token generation) with Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Fallback
        • Autonomous clinical tool-calling: dynamically triggers FDA searches, biomarker lookups, and patient history retrieval.
        • Responsible OTC Pharmacy Guidance: suggests safe over-the-counter tablets (Paracetamol, Ibuprofen) with exact dosing precautions and red-flag alerts.
        • Dynamic Trending Health Topics: Rotates real-world health queries on reload and shuffle.
        • Context-Aware Follow-Up Chips: Automatically analyzes prior assistant answers to generate intelligent, personalized next questions.

        Clinical Safety & Engineering Standards

        ① Zero-Jargon Translation

        Every finding is parsed into accessible, 6th-to-8th grade reading level English, eliminating frightening medical jargon while maintaining diagnostic fidelity.

        ② Strict Factual Grounding

        All drug, dosage, and diagnostic information is grounded directly in live NIH MedlinePlus and FDA APIs to eliminate hallucinations.

        ③ Physician-in-the-Loop

        PatientPulse AI serves as an assistive triage and decision support layer, explicitly empowering and encouraging patient consultations with licensed medical doctors.

        ④ Audit Trail & Cloud Sync

        All diagnostic sessions, transcriptions, and triage determinations are logged to Cloud Supabase PostgreSQL with local SQLite backup resilience.
